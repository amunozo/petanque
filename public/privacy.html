Privacy policy

Pétanque — Village Square · Effective date: 2026-10-08

In short: offline play sends nothing. If you choose online play, your nickname and a random ID are sent to our game server for the match, then deleted automatically. No accounts, no chat, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What we collect

Offline play (practice, against the computer, or two players on one phone) collects nothing and sends nothing: the game does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information. The only time any data leaves your device is if you choose the optional online play described below. There are no accounts or sign-ins, no chat, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

Your preferences (for example sound on/off, language, computer difficulty, match length, and any game-feel tuning values you change) are saved only on your device, in your browser's local storage. They never leave it, including when you play online. Clearing the app's or browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, removes them.

To work offline, the app also keeps a copy of its own files (code, fonts, 3D models) in your device's cache. These are the same files everyone gets; they contain nothing about you.

Online play (optional)

Only if you use “Play a friend online”, the following is sent to our game server: the nickname you choose, a random identifier generated on your device (it is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or advertising ID), and your throws. This is needed so the match can be played and so you can rejoin it after a dropped connection. Your opponent sees your nickname, so please do not use your real name if you would rather not share it.

The game server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which acts as our service provider (data processor), at petanque-server.amunozo-gamedev.workers.dev.

Match rooms and this data are deleted automatically when the room expires: at most about 2 hours after the last activity, or 30 minutes in a lobby. There are no accounts and no chat, and the data is not used for anything else.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share, sell, or give any data to third parties, and nothing is used for advertising. The only outside service involved is Cloudflare, which runs the online-play server for us as our service provider. The game contains no third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics services.

Hosting

The game is delivered as a web app from a static web host. Like any web server, the host may keep routine technical logs (such as IP address and request time) under its own privacy policy. We do not have access to these logs for the purpose of identifying players, and we do not use them. Likewise, when you play online, Cloudflare processes IP addresses to deliver the connection; we do not store them.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. It may use your device's vibration (haptic feedback) during play if you have it enabled.

Children

Offline, the game collects no personal data, and online play uses only a nickname and a random identifier (no accounts, no chat). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; young players should choose a nickname that is not their real name.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Comme tout serveur web, l’hébergeur peut conserver des journaux techniques courants (adresse IP, heure de la requête, par exemple) selon sa propre politique de confidentialité. Hors ligne, le jeu ne collecte aucune donnée personnelle, et le jeu en ligne n’utilise qu’un pseudo et un identifiant aléatoire (ni compte ni chat). Nous ne collectons pas sciemment d’informations personnelles auprès d’enfants ; les jeunes joueurs devraient choisir un pseudo qui n’est pas leur vrai nom.
